## Method selection

| Situation | Method | Discriminating output |
|---|---|---|
| An upstream result does not meet a downstream interface | Bridge lemma and smallest end-to-end fixture | Explicit assumptions and a checked conversion between the two contracts |
| Each path is accurate but the ensemble is unstable | Stratification or analytic control variate | Raw estimator versus candidate at equal cost on independent evaluation samples |
| Reset, locality or history may control the result | Paired falsification with fixed observable support | Change only the suspect assumption and resolve its effect |
| A derivation may hide circularity or shared errors | Blind re-derivation and adversarial negative controls | Independent route and a counterexample that the evaluator correctly rejects |
| Verified computation is expensive | Small code-candidate search with independent evaluator | Correctness before speed; measured cost on the same problem |
| Apparatus inputs do not determine the observable | Input ledger and identifiability test | Missing independent measurement or a bounded conditional prediction |
| A promising result is hard to understand or reuse | Simplify the mechanism into a reduced lemma | Short derivation with the assumptions that carry the result |

For the chosen method record a question, what result changes the decision, the
existing control/reference, changed axis, expected new/reused work and stopping
condition in the current card. Preserve alternatives only while they answer
different hypotheses. Candidate and reviewer counts are operational defaults,
not fixed human limits.

## ⟦AI⟧ Current GC candidates

### Ensemble integration

Preserve the incoming-flux measure, density counted once, every RF/source
complex matrix and signed cancellation. Residence bins need analytic masses or
independently converged probability integrals; fixed partitions in the original
Sobol coordinates can have known masses.

For uniform stationary ballistic first-exit transport, independently derive
`integral tau dLambda = nV`. A matrix control variate uses
`I[F] = I[F - C*tau] + C*nV`. Freeze C independently of evaluation samples and
preserve ordering, conjugacy, phase selection and source-sum identities. A
scramble spread comparison measures stability; a true-error claim needs its
own error argument. P3/811 includes p2/811: training on p2/811 requires a new
evaluation seed. Save the candidate estimator separately from historical sums.

### Atomic to optical bridge

Start with one path/small basis. Specify mean polarization, causal response,
spatial noise correlations, frequency pairing and photon-flux normalization.
Compare kernel propagation with an independent direct integral equation and
no-atom, zero-length, passive and physical temporal-mode controls. Use the
existing WP-OPTICAL dependency rather than creating another master plan.

### Independent validation and learning

Give the blind reviewer inputs, target and assumptions before the proposed
formula. Use false density counting, missing Poisson mean outer, deleted source
correlations or wrong sideband conventions as negative controls where relevant.
Retain failed hypotheses with their smallest counterexample and reopening
condition. Seek a simpler mechanism once a result is promising.

### Input preparation

units, covariance, use and acquisition route. If two allowed models under the
same recorded inputs yield different S_minus, identify the needed independent
measurement. An explicitly requested fitted diagnostic can be developed under
its own result type while the no-fit milestone keeps its registered meaning.
